TOUCH & DRAG TO REVEAL THE ALTER EGO

I grew up in Cairo, studied graphic design there, and spent eight years in studios in Cairo, Amsterdam and Copenhagen before going independent in 2022.

Most of my clients are small teams with one good product and no time to explain it. I work with them directly, usually alone, sometimes with a photographer, developer or copywriter I trust.

Outside client work I collect bus timetables and badly translated signs. That is where most of my opinions about wayfinding come from.
